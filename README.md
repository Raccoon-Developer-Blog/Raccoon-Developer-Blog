# Ivan Raccoon-Developer-Blog

## IC Design Engineer | Microelectronics | Game Development Enthusiast

### About Me

I am an IC Design Engineer with over 9 years of experience in microelectronics and integrated circuit development.

I graduated from Bauman Moscow State Technical University (Kaluga Branch) with a Bachelor's degree in Electronic Equipment Design and Manufacturing. Since 2017, I have been working in the field of integrated circuit development, progressing to the position of Design Engineer (1st Category).

My professional activities include analog and mixed-signal IC design, layout development, transistor characterization, electrical measurements, and semiconductor device analysis. I work with CMOS and bipolar technologies and actively participate in mentoring students and onboarding new engineers.

Alongside my engineering career, I am passionate about software and game development. In my free time, I study modern software architecture, Android development, and game development using Unreal Engine.
